# Code of conduct

Contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful, specific and constructive.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate humiliation and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Review code and ideas rather than attacking people. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that violates these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable for contributors.

Good-faith disagreement about implementation, scope and architecture is welcome.
